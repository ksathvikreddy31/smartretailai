Refund & Cancellation

Cancellations: Allowed only before the order status changes to 'Shipped'.
Refund Timeline: Once a return is picked up and inspected, refunds are initiated within 48 hours.
Mode of Refund: 
- Credit/Debit Cards: 5-7 business days.
- UPI: Instant.
- SmartRetailAI Wallet: Instant.
Damaged Goods: If an electronic or glass item arrives broken, please upload a photo within 24 hours for an immediate refund.
